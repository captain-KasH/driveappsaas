# Product vision — Taxios

Taxios is a transport operating system that lets independent transport businesses in Sri Lanka run
their own branded ride-hailing service without building or maintaining a software stack. It then
grows into a shared consumer marketplace and an enterprise mobility platform.

## What Taxios is
1. **Operator OS** — dispatch, drivers, vehicles, fares, trips, payments records and reporting for one business.
2. **SaaS infrastructure** — many operators on one platform, strictly isolated.
3. **Shared marketplace** — a consumer channel where participating operators offer rides under their own identity.
4. **Add-on marketplace** — paid modules: communications, AI, payments, growth, fleet, corporate, analytics, enterprise.
5. **Enterprise product** — dedicated or self-hosted deployments under licence.

## Who it serves
Taxi companies, three-wheeler fleets, independent drivers and small fleets, airport transfer,
chauffeur and premium services, tourism transfers, hotel transport desks, corporate employee
transport, scheduled shuttles, new transport brands, and large fleets. Service categories are
configurable per jurisdiction — Taxios never assumes a vehicle type is legal everywhere.

## Commercial models
| Model | What the customer gets |
|---|---|
| A. Hosted SaaS | Shared platform, plans + add-ons, we run infra, upgrades, backups |
| B. White-label SaaS | Branded passenger/driver apps, booking site, custom domain on the shared platform |
| C. Dedicated enterprise | Isolated deployment, negotiated resources, integrations, support, controls |
| D. Self-hosted binary licence | Licensed artifacts run on customer infrastructure |
| E. Full-source licence | Contractual source access; **source access ≠ IP ownership**, redistribution, sublicensing or resale rights |

Commercial models stay independent of the core booking implementation.

## Principles
- Working, verified software over breadth. One real end-to-end booking before more modules.
- Tenant isolation, financial correctness and honest UI (no button pretends something happened) are non-negotiable.
- AI is optional and assistive; core booking and dispatch work without it.
- Sri Lanka first (LKR, Sinhala/Tamil/English, local providers), built to expand internationally.
